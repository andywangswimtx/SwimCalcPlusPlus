

<h1 align="center">SwimCalc++ [Age 13 and over]</h1>

Built for high school swimmers chasing NCAA roster spots (D1, D2, and D3), and optimized for quick, easy use right at the pool deck. Start unlocking your college potential today — **100% FREE, no catch!**

SwimCalc++ is a performance tracker and reference tool designed primarily for high school competitive swimmers, their parents, and coaches. Unlike other performance trackers, SwimCalc++ focuses on analyzing a swimmer's performance in a single race rather than tracking progress over time.

After manually inputting just one race time, the app instantly compares your performance against a wide range of swimming standards ("cuts"), including:

- USA Swimming Motivational Time Standards
- Chinese National Swim Standards
- Futures
- Winter Juniors
- Junior Nationals
- TYR Pro Championships
- U.S. Open
- Olympic Trials
- LA2028 Olympic swimming qualifying times
- Texas LSC standards (currently supported), including TAGS and TSC Sectionals and much more

The app also calculates your Swimcloud® Power Index for each event, a widely recognized performance metric used by many NCAA college swimming programs during recruiting.

In addition, SwimCalc++ helps you:
- Calculate the time improvement needed to reach your target goal.
- Compare your performance with multiple qualifying standards in seconds.
- Plan realistic performance goals for upcoming meets.

SwimCalc++ works entirely offline. No account, no login, and internet connection is required.
